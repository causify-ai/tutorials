### Project 1: Understanding the Impact of Education on Income

- **Difficulty**: 1
- **Tech Description**: DoWhy will be used to create and validate causal inference models to understand the relationship between education level and income.
- **Project Idea**: The goal of this project is to analyze the causal effect of education on individual income using observational data from the National Longitudinal Survey of Youth (NLSY). Students will formulate a causal graph to identify confounding variables and use DoWhy to estimate the causal effect while controlling for these variables. The project will provide insights into how education influences income, allowing for policy recommendations related to education funding.
- **Python libs**: DoWhy, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Statsmodels
- **Is it Free?**: Yes, the NLSY dataset is publicly available and can be accessed without any costs.
- **Relevant tool (DoWhy) related Resource Links**: 
  - [DoWhy Documentation](https://microsoft.github.io/dowhy/)
  - [Causal Inference with DoWhy](https://towardsdatascience.com/causal-inference-with-dowhy-in-python-9c0a0e9c1a2f)

### Project 2: Evaluating the Effect of Marketing Campaigns on Sales

- **Difficulty**: 2
- **Tech Description**: DoWhy will be employed to assess the causal impact of marketing strategies on product sales using observational data.
- **Project Idea**: This project aims to evaluate the effectiveness of different marketing campaigns on sales performance for a retail company using historical sales data from Kaggle. Students will construct a causal graph to identify potential confounders, and then apply DoWhy to estimate the causal effects of various marketing strategies. The findings will help the company optimize future marketing efforts based on data-driven insights.
- **Python libs**: DoWhy, Pandas, NumPy, Seaborn, Scikit-learn
- **Is it Free?**: Yes, the dataset is available on Kaggle for free and can be downloaded without any subscription.
- **Relevant tool (DoWhy) related Resource Links**: 
  - [DoWhy Documentation](https://microsoft.github.io/dowhy/)
  - [Causal Inference in Marketing](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2020/03/understanding-causal-inference-in-marketing/)

### Project 3: Assessing the Impact of Air Quality on Public Health

- **Difficulty**: 3
- **Tech Description**: DoWhy will be utilized to estimate the causal effects of air quality on health outcomes using complex observational data.
- **Project Idea**: This advanced project will investigate the causal relationship between air pollution levels (PM2.5) and respiratory health outcomes using data from the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and CDC. Students will create a comprehensive causal model to account for various confounders, such as socioeconomic status and geographical factors. By applying DoWhy, they will estimate the causal impact of air quality on health, contributing to public health policy discussions and interventions aimed at improving air quality.
- **Python libs**: DoWhy, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Statsmodels, GeoPandas
- **Is it Free?**: Yes, both the EPA air quality data and CDC health outcomes data are publicly available at no cost.
- **Relevant tool (DoWhy) related Resource Links**: 
  - [DoWhy Documentation](https://microsoft.github.io/dowhy/)
  - [Causal Inference in Public Health](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6462702/)
