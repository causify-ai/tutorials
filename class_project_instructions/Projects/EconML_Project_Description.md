### Project 1: Analyzing the Impact of Online Advertising on Sales
- **Difficulty**: 1
- **Tech Description**: EconML will be used to estimate the causal impact of online advertising on sales using observational data.
- **Project Idea**: This project aims to analyze how online advertising campaigns influence sales in a retail environment. By leveraging a dataset containing sales figures and advertising spend across various channels, students will apply EconML's causal inference techniques to estimate the treatment effect of advertising on sales. The analysis will help identify the most effective advertising channels and optimize future marketing strategies.
- **Python libs**: EconML, pandas, statsmodels, matplotlib, seaborn
- **Is it Free?**: Yes, EconML is an open-source library available for free.
- **Relevant tool (EconML) related Resource Links**: [EconML Documentation](https://econml.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), [EconML GitHub Repository](https://github.com/microsoft/EconML)

### Project 2: Evaluating the Effectiveness of Educational Interventions
- **Difficulty**: 2
- **Tech Description**: EconML will be utilized to analyze the causal effects of different educational programs on student performance.
- **Project Idea**: This project focuses on evaluating the effectiveness of various educational interventions on student test scores using public data from the National Center for Education Statistics (NCES). Students will implement causal inference methods in EconML to determine how different teaching methods affect learning outcomes. The results will provide insights into which interventions yield the highest improvements in student performance, informing policy decisions in education.
- **Python libs**: EconML, pandas, scikit-learn, matplotlib, statsmodels
- **Is it Free?**: Yes, both the dataset from NCES and EconML are freely available.
- **Relevant tool (EconML) related Resource Links**: [EconML Documentation](https://econml.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), [NCES Data](https://nces.ed.gov)

### Project 3: Understanding the Impact of Policy Changes on Employment Rates
- **Difficulty**: 3
- **Tech Description**: EconML will be employed to perform advanced causal analysis of policy changes on employment rates over time.
- **Project Idea**: This project aims to investigate the causal effects of recent labor policy changes on employment rates across different sectors. Utilizing time-series data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), students will apply EconML's advanced techniques for heterogeneous treatment effects to assess how different sectors respond to policy changes. The analysis will involve complex modeling to account for confounding variables and will provide valuable insights for policymakers and economists.
- **Python libs**: EconML, pandas, statsmodels, matplotlib, numpy
- **Is it Free?**: Yes, the BLS data and EconML are both publicly accessible at no cost.
- **Relevant tool (EconML) related Resource Links**: [EconML Documentation](https://econml.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), [Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov)
