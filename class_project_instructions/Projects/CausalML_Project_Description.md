### Project 1: Evaluating Marketing Campaign Effectiveness
- **Difficulty**: 1
- **Tech Description**: CausalML is used to estimate the causal impact of a marketing campaign on customer behavior using observational data.
- **Project Idea**: The goal of this project is to analyze the effectiveness of a recent marketing campaign on customer purchase behavior. Using transaction data from an e-commerce platform, the student will apply causal inference techniques to determine whether the campaign significantly increased sales compared to a control group. The project will involve data cleaning, selection of relevant features, and the application of CausalML to derive insights and actionable recommendations for future campaigns.
- **Python libs**: CausalML, pandas, numpy, matplotlib, scikit-learn
- **Is it Free?**: Yes, CausalML is open-source and freely available for use.
- **Relevant tool (CausalML) related Resource Links**: [CausalML GitHub](https://github.com/uber/causalml), [CausalML Documentation](https://causalml.readthedocs.io/en/latest/)

### Project 2: Assessing the Impact of Education Programs on Student Performance
- **Difficulty**: 2
- **Tech Description**: CausalML is utilized to evaluate the causal effects of educational interventions on student performance metrics.
- **Project Idea**: This project aims to investigate the impact of a new educational program implemented in various schools on student performance in standardized tests. The student will gather publicly available data from educational databases, such as the National Center for Education Statistics (NCES). Using CausalML, the student will employ techniques like propensity score matching to create comparable groups and measure the causal effect of the program on test scores, providing insights into the program's efficacy.
- **Python libs**: CausalML, statsmodels, pandas, seaborn, scikit-learn
- **Is it Free?**: Yes, both CausalML and the educational datasets are freely available for public use.
- **Relevant tool (CausalML) related Resource Links**: [CausalML GitHub](https://github.com/uber/causalml), [NCES Data Tools](https://nces.ed.gov/datatools/)

### Project 3: Analyzing the Impact of Air Quality on Public Health Outcomes
- **Difficulty**: 3
- **Tech Description**: CausalML is leveraged to analyze the causal relationship between air quality metrics and health outcomes using longitudinal data.
- **Project Idea**: This advanced project focuses on exploring the causal effects of air quality on public health outcomes, specifically respiratory diseases. The student will utilize air quality data from the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and health outcome data from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC). By applying advanced causal inference methods in CausalML, such as instrumental variable approaches, the student will analyze the data to quantify the impact of air pollution on hospital admission rates for respiratory issues, helping to inform public health policies.
- **Python libs**: CausalML, pandas, numpy, matplotlib, statsmodels
- **Is it Free?**: Yes, both CausalML and the datasets from the EPA and CDC are publicly accessible without cost.
- **Relevant tool (CausalML) related Resource Links**: [CausalML GitHub](https://github.com/uber/causalml), [EPA Air Quality Data](https://www.epa.gov/outdoor-air-quality-data), [CDC Data](https://data.cdc.gov/)
