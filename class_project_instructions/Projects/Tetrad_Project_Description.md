### Project 1: Causal Analysis of Air Quality and Health Outcomes
- **Difficulty:** 1
- **Tech Description:** Tetrad will be used to analyze causal relationships between air quality metrics and health outcomes using graphical models.
- **Project Idea:** This project aims to investigate the causal impact of various air quality indicators (e.g., PM2.5, NO2) on respiratory health outcomes (e.g., asthma rates). By utilizing publicly available datasets from the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), the student will construct causal diagrams using Tetrad. The analysis will involve identifying direct and indirect causal relationships and assessing the strength of these connections, providing insights into public health policies.
- **Python libs:** pandas, numpy, matplotlib, Tetrad
- **Is it Free?** Yes, Tetrad is open-source and can be freely downloaded and used for causal modeling.
- **Relevant tool (Tetrad) related Resource Links:** [Tetrad Documentation](http://www.phil.cmu.edu/tetrad/), [Tetrad GitHub Repository](https://github.com/cmu-phil/tetrad)

### Project 2: Understanding the Causal Factors of Consumer Behavior
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Tech Description:** Tetrad will be employed to model and analyze causal relationships among various consumer behavior metrics using observational data.
- **Project Idea:** This project seeks to explore the causal factors influencing consumer purchasing decisions through an analysis of e-commerce data from Kaggle. The student will gather data on customer demographics, product characteristics, and purchasing history. By applying Tetrad, the student will construct a causal model to identify which factors most significantly drive consumer behavior. The findings will be used to recommend strategies for targeted marketing and product placement.
- **Python libs:** pandas, seaborn, statsmodels, Tetrad
- **Is it Free?** Yes, all tools and datasets used in this project are freely available for academic use.
- **Relevant tool (Tetrad) related Resource Links:** [Tetrad Documentation](http://www.phil.cmu.edu/tetrad/), [Kaggle E-commerce Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets)

### Project 3: Causal Discovery in Climate Change and Crop Yields
- **Difficulty:** 3
- **Tech Description:** Tetrad will be utilized for causal discovery to analyze the relationships between climate factors and agricultural yield data.
- **Project Idea:** This advanced project aims to uncover the causal relationships between climate change variables (temperature, precipitation) and crop yields using historical agricultural data from the FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization). The student will use Tetrad to perform causal discovery, identifying key climate factors that impact crop yields over time. By integrating time-series analysis, the project will provide insights into potential future agricultural strategies under changing climate conditions.
- **Python libs:** pandas, numpy, matplotlib, Tetrad, statsmodels
- **Is it Free?** Yes, both Tetrad and the FAO datasets are freely accessible for research purposes.
- **Relevant tool (Tetrad) related Resource Links:** [Tetrad Documentation](http://www.phil.cmu.edu/tetrad/), [FAO Data Repository](http://www.fao.org/faostat/en/)
