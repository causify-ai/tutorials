**Title:** Analyzing Economic Indicators to Predict Stock Market Trends

**Difficulty:** 2

**Tech Description:** Statsmodels will be used to perform time series analysis and regression modeling on economic indicators to forecast stock market trends.

**Project Idea:** This project aims to analyze various economic indicators, such as unemployment rates, GDP growth, and inflation rates, to predict stock market trends using historical stock prices. By leveraging publicly available data from the Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) API, we will collect and preprocess the data, then apply time series regression techniques using Statsmodels to identify relationships between economic indicators and stock market movements. The goal is to create a predictive model that can provide insights into future stock market performance based on economic conditions.

**Python libs:** statsmodels, pandas, numpy, matplotlib, requests

**Is it Free?** Yes
